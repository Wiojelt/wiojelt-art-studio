The idea

Strangers, without the noise.

Most social apps ask for a polished identity before a single hello. We want to test a simpler starting point: a brief conversation between two people who choose to be there.

01 / Meet

No public profile

Start with a random introduction. Later matches could include a short, opt-in conversation summary, giving context without a permanent public persona.

02 / Choose

Both say yes

Each person accepts the match before the conversation opens. Either can leave. The experience is designed around consent, clear boundaries, and reporting.

03 / Talk

15 minutes to start

A small daily time budget encourages a real exchange. The first version focuses on typed messages, with no links or media.

The Claude plan

Language understanding where it counts.

Claude is planned for the moments where simple rules miss context. These are workflows to test in a private pilot, not features already running on this page.

01 / Safety

Review risky behavior

Surface likely harassment, spam, and attempts to share contact details for human review. We would evaluate false positives before relying on model decisions.

02 / Discovery

Offer a starting point

Suggest an opening topic from interests people explicitly choose to share, without requiring a public profile or exposing past chats.

03 / Reflection

Reflect with consent

Explore optional summaries of conversation themes. No personality scores or claims about who a person is.

04 / Learning

Make feedback useful

Group voluntary, anonymous post-chat feedback into themes so the first pilot can improve without naming the people in it.

The product still needs clear consent, short retention, reporting, human review, and a way to appeal mistakes. Those safeguards are part of the build plan, not finished claims.
Where things stand

A concept with a testable first step.

Hi Stranger is currently a product concept and interface prototype. There is no live matching service, active user community, or production Claude integration yet.

The first build would focus on mutual matching, a 15-minute text conversation, leaving and reporting controls, and a small Claude-assisted safety experiment. Paid time extensions and monthly reflections remain hypotheses for later.

Now / Clickable concept

Show the flow, gather feedback, and narrow the first build.

Next / Private pilot

Build matching, timers, reporting, and a human-reviewed safety workflow.

Learn / Measure carefully

Track mutual opt-in, conversation completion, reports, and moderation mistakes before widening access.
